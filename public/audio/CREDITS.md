# Audio credits

All sound effects in `sfx/` are by **Kenney** (https://kenney.nl), released under
**CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain). They were taken from Kenney's official
starter kits on GitHub:

- KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-FPS — blaster, blaster_repeater, enemy_attack, enemy_destroy, enemy_hurt, jump_a, land, weapon_change
- KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-3D-Platformer — coin, break, fall
- KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-City-Builder — placement-a, placement-c, removal-a, rotate, toggle
- KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-Racing — skid, impact
- KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-Match-3 — tile-land, tile-match, tile-swap

Background music: drop `.ogg` files into `bgm/` and list them in `bgm/bgm.json`
(keys: `title`, `menu`, `stage`, `boss`), e.g. `{ "stage": "forest.ogg" }`.
