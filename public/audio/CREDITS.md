# Audio credits

All clips were trimmed, normalised and re-encoded to MP3 for this game.

## Music — CC0 1.0
- **Komiku** — "Action Discovery" (`bgm/lobby.mp3`), "Battle Theme" (`bgm/battle.mp3`),
  "Boss 1: The first challenge" (`bgm/boss1.mp3`), "Last Boss: Let's see what we got" (`bgm/boss2.mp3`).
  Via the SoundSafari CC0-1.0-Music collection.

## Sound effects
- **Free Firearm Sound Library** by Ben Jaszczak — CC0 1.0.
  Gunshots and reload foley: `shot_*`, `mag_*`, `slide`, `cyl_*`, `shell_*`, `smg_*`, `pump_*`, `bolt_*`, `clip`, `box_*`, `belt`.
- **Kenney** (kenney.nl) — Impact Sounds, Sci-Fi Sounds, Digital Audio, RPG Audio — CC0 1.0.
  `kill*`, `bhit*`, `slam*`, `eshot*`, `rail`, `charge`, `reflect`, `gem*`, `click`.
- **Beatscribe** — Homebrew VGM jingles — CC0 1.0. `boss`, `start`.
- **"Oculus Audio Pack 01" by Oculus VR, LLC** (https://developers.meta.com/vr/downloads/package/oculus-audio-pack-1/),
  licensed under **CC BY 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Files renamed to the UCS naming
  convention; OGG previews transcoded from the original WAVs. Further trimmed and re-encoded here.
  `parry` / `parry_perfect` are the sword-clash recording pitched up, high-passed and run through a
  struck-blade resonator, layered with a sword-scrape transient and a Kenney light metal tick.
  `swing_*`, `blade`, `parry`, `parry_perfect`, `sheathe`, `hit*`, `hurt`, `casing*`, `ui*`, `deny`, `buy`, `coin`,
  `levelup`, `heal`, `victory`, `defeat`, `dash`.
